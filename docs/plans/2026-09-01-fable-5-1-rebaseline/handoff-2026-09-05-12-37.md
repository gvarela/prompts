## Critical Learnings

### Discoveries Not in Documentation

1. **Barrier volume is not what holds a wait-for-all gate.** On the trap fixture (2/3 reports back, third streaming a near-complete partial) Sonnet synthesized anyway under both `⛔⛔⛔ STOP!` (WAIT 0/3) and a single marker with reason (WAIT 1/3). Recorded in beads memory `wb-barrier-volume-not-discriminator`. A partial-report gate needs a harness mechanism or explicit async-spawn semantics, not louder text.
2. **Scope prohibitions hold; the reporting channel is weak.** Both scope-block wordings left a trap bug unfixed 3/3, but every run surfaced it as an aside after "Issues encountered: None", never as a listed issue.
3. **beads does not auto-flush `issues.jsonl` by default.** The tracked export was 5 weeks stale (92 vs 156 issues) despite CLAUDE.md and `plugin/docs/reference/beads-mode.md` saying otherwise; `bd config set export.auto true` is the switch. Filed as prompts-vwo. Moot for this repo now (stealth mode).
4. **Headless `claude --plugin-dir … -p` executes wb skills reliably** and `--output-format stream-json --verbose` exposes Skill events as `"skill":"wb:<name>"`. Limits: no permission grants (runs stop at the first `bd`/write prompt), macOS has no `timeout` (use the tool timeout or `--max-turns`). Closed prompts-3us on this.
5. **`update_status` is the sole writer of progress fields** (since v2.3.0); it was user-only until v2.6.0, so no phase could reconcile its own status. Fixed by this session's change.
6. **Sonnet 5 effort curves are monotonic through max** (system card §8); the "deteriorates at xhigh" intuition belongs to Sonnet 4.6. Fable 5 at low scores above Sonnet 5 at xhigh on FrontierCode/CursorBench at similar cost — see `thoughts/2026-09-01-effort-curves-and-fable-routing.md` and prompts-yfh.
